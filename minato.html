About the Project

Minato Sushi required a sophisticated brand identity that reflects the premium quality of their cuisine and the authentic Japanese dining experience.

The challenge was to create a visual system that conveys elegance, tradition, and modernity while appealing to discerning food enthusiasts.

From the logo design featuring the iconic wave symbol to the complete brand guidelines, every element was crafted to position Minato Sushi as a premium dining destination.
